Our Purpose

Conservation isn't a line item.
It's part of the itinerary.

Saint Barth Gorilla Expeditions was built on the belief that extraordinary travel and genuine stewardship belong on the same itinerary. The Saint Barth Gorilla Fund is how we make that permanent: a portion of every journey's proceeds is directed to conservation and community partners working where our guests travel.

Guests are invited to meet the people behind the work — visiting a school in Loliondo, sitting with a Maasai women's cooperative, or joining a ranger briefing in the Virunga range — and to give directly, on the ground, to the projects that move them. At home in St Barts, we widen the circle further, hosting gatherings and events to raise additional support for the Fund each year.

The Pledge

Give With Every Journey

A fixed share of profit from each Saint Barth Gorilla Expeditions journey is contributed to the Fund automatically — no guest action required.

The Encounter

Meet the Communities

Guests visit partner schools, cooperatives, and research stations in person — to listen, volunteer an afternoon, and give directly if moved to.

The Gathering

Raise Together in St Barts

Each season we host events on the island — bringing our community together to expand what the Fund can support in the field.

Where the giving goes

Tanzania

Northern Serengeti & Loliondo
Community & Conservation

Serengeti Preservation Foundation

Based in Loliondo, working directly with Maasai communities on women's entrepreneurship, conservation education, sustainable agriculture, and school programs.

Loliondo, Tanzania
Wildlife Conservation

Frankfurt Zoological Society — Serengeti Programme

Major conservation work across the entire Serengeti ecosystem, including anti-poaching, habitat restoration, and predator monitoring in the northern migration corridors.

fzs.org
Research & Science

Tanzania Wildlife Research Institute (TAWIRI)

The Serengeti Wildlife Research Centre in Seronera supports lion, elephant, leopard, and rhino research, GPS tracking programs, and student field science.

tawiri.or.tz
Education

Bright English Medium School (BEMS)

Founded as an orphanage for vulnerable Maasai children in Loliondo, now a full school with boarding facilities, supported through student sponsorship and infrastructure gifts.

Sponsor a student

Rwanda

Volcanoes National Park & the Virunga Range
Wildlife Conservation

Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund

One of the world's leading mountain gorilla conservation organizations, based at the Ellen DeGeneres Campus — museum, research labs, and veterinary medicine programs.

gorillafund.org
Women's Enterprise

Kinigi Women Village

A women-led artisan cooperative near Volcanoes National Park empowering women through weaving, tailoring, beading, and pottery — and fair-trade sales of their work.

kinigiwomenvillage.com
Community & Health

Kwizera Charity Foundation

A Kigali-based nonprofit supporting vulnerable children through education, nutrition, healthcare, counseling, and annual medical outreach for underserved families.

Kigali, Rwanda
Education

Nyabihu School for the Deaf

Provides education, Rwandan Sign Language, and vocational training for deaf children in western Rwanda, many from families with limited access to specialized education.

nsd.rw
Veterinary Care

Gorilla Doctors

Field veterinarians providing direct, hands-on care to wild mountain and Grauer's gorillas across the Virunga and Bwindi ecosystems.

Ruhengeri, Rwanda

Get Involved

Beyond the safari

On the Ground

Every Saint Barth Gorilla Expeditions itinerary can include time with a Fund partner — a school visit, a cooperative workshop, a ranger briefing, or a private conservation talk at your camp.

  • Visit & Volunteer — spend an afternoon at a partner school or cooperative during your journey.
  • Sponsor Directly — fund a specific need from our Field Needs ledger, on the spot or after you return home.
  • Private Talks — arrange an evening presentation from a researcher, vet, or ranger at your lodge.

In St Barts

We host seasonal gatherings on the island — dinners, auctions, and evenings with guest speakers from the field — to widen support for the Fund beyond our travelers.

Give Directly

Support the Fund

Gifts made here go directly to the Saint Barth Gorilla Fund and are allocated to our field partners, prioritizing the most urgent needs unless you designate otherwise.

Every gift, of any size, is felt in the field — a term of school fees, a set of hearing aids, a truck of bricks for a dormitory.
